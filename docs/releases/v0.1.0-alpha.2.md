Second Android prerelease for personal TransMilenio and SITP arrivals boards.

- The app is now named TransMinuto and has a new launcher icon.
- Read published service hours and ordered route stops offline from the first launch.
- Choose route directions and variants with their destinations and schedules kept separate.
- Choose an appearance, reorder boards, and give boards custom names.
- Search the packaged catalog with faster loading.
- Switch between cached boards more smoothly, with a quieter home header and improved initial loading.

Validation: publication requires passing Android unit tests, lint, APK assembly, Android 15 instrumented tests, and Python tooling tests. The release workflow verifies the APK signature and packages SHA-256 checksums.

Known limitations:
- The unofficial live feed can reject requests or return no arrivals. This release does not establish current live-feed availability.
- Published service hours describe operating windows, not the time a bus passes a selected station or stop.
- The FZ63 composite selection is mapped to the verified Z63/Tibanica direction on route 13789. Other composite mappings remain unsupported until verified.
- Android 8.0 or later is required.

This APK uses live data and the permanent release signing key. It updates the previous prerelease in place and preserves its saved boards and settings. Its package is `co.camilo.transmicrone`; boards from development builds ending in `.debug` do not transfer automatically.
