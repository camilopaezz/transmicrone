Third Android prerelease for personal TransMilenio and SITP arrivals boards.

- Floating glass controls and adaptive layouts across the home, search, setup, and settings screens.
- The board title collapses into the header while scrolling and returns when arrivals reach the top. Short lists and touch exploration keep it expanded.
- Pull-to-refresh shows progress above arrivals, and refresh requests made during an active fetch are queued.
- Arrivals update as each selected service finishes loading.
- Search opens with saved service and place shortcuts, and snackbars stay above controls and the keyboard.

Validation: publication requires passing Android unit tests, lint, APK assembly, Android 15 instrumented tests, and Python tooling tests. The release workflow verifies the APK signature and packages SHA-256 checksums.

Known limitations:
- The unofficial live feed can reject requests or return no arrivals. This release does not establish current live-feed availability.
- Published service hours describe operating windows, not the time a bus passes a selected station or stop.
- The FZ63 composite selection is mapped to the verified Z63/Tibanica direction on route 13789. Other composite mappings remain unsupported until verified.
- Android 8.0 or later is required.

This APK uses live data and the permanent release signing key. It updates the previous prerelease in place and preserves its saved boards and settings. Its package is `co.camilo.transmicrone`; boards from development builds ending in `.debug` or isolated PR builds do not transfer automatically.
