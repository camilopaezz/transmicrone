# Android UI

The current app uses Material 3 Expressive, bundled Adwaita Sans, a Spanish UI, and edge-to-edge layouts. Product boundaries are in [app-scope.md](app-scope.md); builds and verification are in [the Android README](../android/README.md).

## Visual direction

Use Apple-inspired glass styling together with Material 3 Expressive. Reuse the shared glass controls for tonal fills, highlighted rims, and backdrop blur. Keep Material typography, dynamic colors, component behavior, motion, and accessible touch targets. Status and error feedback stays compact so arrivals remain the focus; explanations belong in details dialogs. Inline controls use the shared rim and solid tonal fallback to avoid sampling their own content, while floating controls blur the separate backdrop.

## Theme

System, light, and dark modes are available in Ajustes. Android 12+ wallpaper colors are enabled by default and can be disabled. Other devices, and devices with wallpaper colors disabled, use the transit-red palette. Settings persist separately from saved boards.

`MaterialExpressiveTheme` supplies the motion scheme. App shapes range from 12 to 32 dp. Medium-weight headings and emphasized destination and operating-time styles establish the text hierarchy. Font licenses are bundled with the app.

Service chips and arrival badges use the saved catalog route color, falling back to the live color. All service chips and badges share a translucent route tint and subtle highlighted rim, including home, arrivals, search, details, and service selection. Label contrast is calculated against the tint composited onto the actual screen or card surface, including selected service rows. Inline chips do not sample the backdrop that contains them. Existing pill and badge shapes, typography, and padding are preserved. Badge text is black or white according to luminance. Theme tests cover text contrast for the fallback palettes and tonal containers.

## Home and board selection

The home screen uses separate floating capsules for the brand and search/settings actions over the base surface. A centered floating dock holds edit and add-board actions. Scrolling down through arrivals contracts the add action to its icon, leaving two 48 dp targets. Scrolling up, reaching either end of the list, or changing boards expands it. A 10 dp direction threshold avoids flicker, and pull-to-refresh does not contract the dock. The label fades and the capsule resizes with Material Expressive motion. Touch exploration and disabled system animations keep the label visible. Capsules use a Material tonal tint over a clipped 20 dp backdrop blur, with a lit rim brighter at the top and faint at the bottom. Android 11 and earlier use a stronger translucent tint when backdrop blur is unavailable. The header wraps at large font scales, and the arrivals list has bottom clearance so its last item can scroll above the dock. In windows shorter than 480 dp, or with less than 360 dp of effective height after font scaling, add/edit move into the header and the board title scrolls with the arrivals, leaving the list clear of the dock. Snackbars use the same glass treatment with extra-large corners, a minimum height of 52 dp, and no nested elevated surface. Messages wrap beside 48 dp action and dismiss targets. The Material host retains announcement, timeout, and accessibility dismissal behavior. Snackbars sit 12 dp above floating docks and action panels and account for the keyboard separately from screen content insets. An empty installation shows an add-board action; startup waits for saved boards to load.

The station title starts above the arrivals and moves with the horizontal board pager. Names slide, scale, and fade during gestures; long names ellipsize. Neighboring title hints, a progress line, a text page count, and a temporary swipe hint make additional boards visible. Tap or hold the title, or tap the page count, to open a frosted Material bottom sheet with full board names, locations, selected services, and an add action. The selected board retains a solid secondary-container fill. The sheet shares the arrivals backdrop and keeps Material dismissal and scrolling behavior.

Paging persists selection only after settling. Board IDs preserve each board's vertical scroll position. Upward scrolling collapses the title selector before moving arrivals when the list can scroll. Short lists keep it expanded; live updates that make a list fit restore it after scrolling ends. The large title fades by 45% collapse and the page row by 70%, finishing earlier when either row reaches the clip edge. Transparent controls leave accessibility navigation. Returning to the top expands it before a new pull can refresh. The header crossfades to the board name and page count after 60% collapse; tapping it opens board selection. Switching to a board at its top expands the title. Touch exploration keeps it expanded and uses a list viewport below the fixed title, so scrolled or focused arrivals stay visible. Selected services scroll with arrivals. A progressive blur band softens arrivals passing beneath it after scrolling.

Pull-to-refresh requires a new downward gesture that starts at the top of the list with the title fully expanded, even if the preceding scroll animation is still settling. Fling momentum and the remainder of a return-to-top drag cannot arm refresh. Scrolling back to the top does not refresh. During a pull the list slides down to reveal a 48 dp glass capsule. A manual refresh holds the 72 dp gap at the top until the request ends. Once the list is scrolled, the capsule floats over arrivals without shifting the list or its top blur band. Quiet background refreshes never move the list. Nothing draws above the arrivals viewport. The capsule passes touches through. Active request progress takes precedence over pull-distance feedback and stays visible while scrolling. Switching to a cached board fetches quietly; an uncached first fetch shows decorative skeleton cards. The home header omits fetch age and a retry action. Empty and unsupported requests have distinct empty-list messages. Failed requests show a compact glass-styled status row using the shared control rim and inline tonal fallback before arrivals with a small error icon and a 48 dp retry action. A chevron marks the message as tappable. It opens a scrollable Material details dialog with the explanation, one colored badge and destination per failed service, each service’s last successful fetch time, and retry and close actions. Partial failures use “Actualización incompleta”. Previous arrivals remain visible. The row shows the age of the oldest cached result among the failed services; details retain individual fetch times. Fetch age does not describe the age of vehicle position reports. The row replaces the empty-list card when no arrivals are available, preserves its title and size during a retry with progress inside the tonal retry button, and clears after recovery. Unresolved per-service failures survive cancellation and screen changes until that service returns a successful or unsupported outcome, or the saved selection changes. Retries from the row or dialog keep arrivals in place. Other manual refreshes retain the pull capsule, which stops drawing as soon as a request or pull ends while its gap finishes closing. Search, setup, and catalog request failures share this compact treatment; unavailable catalog routes remain a neutral message. The unofficial-feed notice follows the list.

Arrival cards show service, destination, the cleaned server ETA phrase, and occupancy. Vehicle cards also show fleet label and distance. Reports older than two minutes show `Reporte antiguo` above the last estimate. Individual source timestamps and ages are omitted. Missing occupancy remains unknown; spacing and metric-unit cleanup preserve the stored feed phrase.

Search, place/service details, setup, and settings share the home capsule treatment. All floating header labels, including the home brand, use the same Material title-medium typography. Headers have 16 dp of space above their controls and 4 dp below, in addition to the system status inset. The home board title has an additional 8 dp top inset. Back navigation has its own 52 dp circle beside a title capsule that grows for wrapped text. Search replaces the title capsule with a glass search field beside the back button, both in Buscar and in the place step of board setup. Opening Buscar from home morphs the search/settings capsule into that field; the icons fade out first and the field fades in as the capsule settles. Setup place search does not morph. The field stays available while results scroll and preserves clear, keyboard search, and automatic focus behavior. Shared scroll-edge bands soften content beneath floating headers and bottom controls with a progressive 20 dp blur and a surface gradient that is opaque at the outer edge. Blur falls to zero halfway into each band, keeping stretched source-edge pixels beneath the stronger tint. The top band starts below the status inset and extends 20 dp past the measured header. Bottom bands follow the panel height, or the home dock height plus its safe inset and spacing. They disappear with inline actions and the editing keyboard. Scrollable screens fade bands with list scrollability. Android 11 and earlier use only the gradient. Bands draw outside the content source and pass touches through. Home keeps the scaffold top band off while the title or page row is visible. The arrivals viewport extends behind the header with scrollable clearance for the header and remaining title height. Its measured list size stays fixed through collapse and refresh. At 70% collapse, scrolled arrivals use the measured header band instead of the separate arrival band. Compact windows and touch exploration keep the scaffold top band off. A separate 28 dp progressive blur beneath the board selector softens the arrivals viewport after scrolling while the header band is off, using its own list backdrop. It disappears at the top of the list. The refresh capsule sits in the gap the list opens above that band. Only the current pager page draws this band; the bottom dock keeps its blur band. Search, setup, settings, and detail lists scroll behind the transparent header; its initial clearance is list content padding rather than a fixed opaque strip. Place details and board editing use detached glass action panels. Their lists extend behind the panels and the transparent surrounding area; bottom clearance is scrollable content padding so the last row can move above the actions. Save-panel status text has an additional 12 dp horizontal and 4 dp vertical inset inside the panel. These inline panels use a solid tonal fill, avoiding a blur that would read its own source. Shared safe insets protect headers, lists, and panels from side cutouts. Search consumes the navigation inset before applying keyboard padding.

## Setup, editing, and deletion

Setup has two steps: choose a place, then select services. Search focuses automatically and supports the keyboard search action. Results show place type and disambiguating information; stops sharing name and address also show catalog codes.

When adding, back from service selection returns to the retained search query. When editing, back returns home without saving. Choosing the same place restores the draft selection; a different place resets it. An optional board name is trimmed, and a blank name uses the station or stop name.

Each service row is one accessible toggle. Selected rows use the secondary-container color. Editing initially lists saved services first and preserves that order while toggling. The bottom save action names the selected-service count and requires at least one selection.

The pencil opens the current board's editor. `Cambiar` opens place search with the edit draft retained. Saving returns home with the updated board selected. `Eliminar tablero` sits beneath save and confirms the saved board's name. Deletion works even when the catalog no longer contains its place, selects a remaining neighbor, and offers undo. Undo restores that board while retaining subsequent edits and selection changes. Ajustes can reorder boards with accessible up/down controls.

## Catalog details

Place details list serving services and can start a board. Service details show a direction selector, service badge and stop count, destination and origin, published hours, and ordered route stops. Hours and routes come from the packaged catalog and work offline.

For two route variants in windows with at least 480 dp of effective height after font scaling, the direction selector sits in a floating glass bottom dock and remains available while stops scroll. Its safe bottom inset and 16 dp outer spacing match the home dock; list content can scroll underneath and the final stop can clear the dock. In shorter windows, at larger text sizes, or with more variants, it stays inline with a solid tonal fill. Selection retains Material toggle motion and radio-button semantics.

The direction selector uses one shared track with 6 dp padding and 4 dp gaps. The floating dock has a pill-shaped track and pill segments. Inline selectors use 32 dp outer and 28 dp segment corners so wrapped labels remain readable at large text sizes. Two equal-width choices fit each row. `Hacia` sits above the destination; rows share a height of at least 64 dp and grow for wrapped text. Selection uses the secondary-container fill and radio-button semantics. Duplicate destination names include a variant number. Switching variants updates the header, hours, and route together.

## Accessibility and freshness

Interactive targets are at least 48 dp. Containers grow with font scale. Board sheets and accessibility labels expose full names when visible titles ellipsize. Arrival descriptions include service, destination, ETA, occupancy, and the vehicle or service estimate caveat.

The visible board polls every 25 seconds. Successful manual refreshes have an eight-second throttle; failed requests can retry immediately, and an in-flight guard prevents duplicate requests. Fetch time and vehicle source time remain separate. The UI marks known vehicle reports older than two minutes as stale and does not derive a precise countdown.

Ajustes also explains arrival ranges, old reports, and unknown occupancy, and shows the app version and unofficial data-source notice. There is no in-app credential or sample-data toggle.
